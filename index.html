Privacy Notice

Version 2026-11-01 · Last updated 1 November 2026

M/s Shivam Steel Works (SSW) (“we”, “us”) runs this app and website for wholesale dealers. This notice explains what personal data we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and how you can control it. We process your data only with your consent, or where the law allows.

What we collect and why

Name and mobile number

Data: Your name and mobile number (and email, if you add one).

Purpose: To create and verify your account with an OTP, to contact you about your account and orders, and to decide whether to approve you as a dealer. If you delete your account we keep your name and mobile number for up to 3 years (see “How long we keep it”).

Business details

Data: Business name, owner name, GST number, and PAN (optional).

Purpose: To verify you as a dealer and to prepare GST invoices.

Delivery addresses

Data: Contact name, mobile number and address for each delivery address you save.

Purpose: To deliver your orders. We do not collect your phone’s GPS location.

Orders, cart, wishlist and searches

Data: Items you order, add to cart or save, order notes, preferred delivery date, and recent searches.

Purpose: To process, bill and deliver orders, to let you reorder, and to show your recent searches.

Notifications

Data: A device token that lets us send notifications to your phone.

Purpose: To send order updates and account approval. Offers and announcements are sent only if you turn them on (off by default).

Website enquiry form

Data: Business name, contact name, mobile number, and optionally email, city and message.

Purpose: To contact you about your wholesale enquiry. We keep it until we have replied and for up to 12 months after.

Language choice

Data: English or Hindi.

Purpose: To show the app in your language.

Who else receives your data

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that work for us: our cloud hosting and database providers, the SMS service that delivers OTPs, and Google Firebase for notifications. They may only use it to provide that service. We may also share data when the law requires it. Your data may be stored on servers outside India; we use providers that protect it with security controls.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is active. If you delete your account we erase your profile, business details, addresses, cart, wishlist, searches, notifications and device tokens. We keep only a small record of who you were (your name, your mobile number, and the dates your account was created and deleted) for up to 3 years after deletion, and then it is removed automatically. We use it only to match past orders, payments and disputes to the right person, to prevent fraud and misuse, and to deal with legal claims. Your orders also carry the name and mobile number on your account at the time you placed them. We must keep order and billing records for tax and accounting laws (generally up to 8 years); these are kept only for that purpose. Security logs are kept for one year.

Your rights

You can see and download your data, correct it, withdraw your consent, ask us to erase it, and nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act. In the app, go to Account > Privacy & data. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it; deleting your account ends the service because we cannot run your account without this data. When you ask us to erase your data we erase everything except the records described under “How long we keep it”. We reply within 30 days.

Complaints

Contact our grievance officer first: The Proprietor, ssw571978@gmail.com, +91 88408 18280. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India (www.dpb.gov.in).

Age

This app is for business owners and staff aged 18 or above. Do not use it if you are under 18.

Security and breaches

We protect data with encryption in transit, access controls and limited staff access. If a breach affects your data we will tell you what happened, what it may mean for you and what we are doing, and we will inform the Data Protection Board.

Changes to this notice

If we change what we collect or why, we will show you the new notice and ask for your consent again.
